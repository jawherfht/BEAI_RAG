Acme Warranty Terms

All products carry the legal 2-year warranty from the date of delivery. Acme-brand furniture has an extended 5-year warranty on frames. The warranty does not cover normal wear, misuse or damage from incorrect cleaning. Warranty claims need the order number and photos of the defect. Approved claims are repaired first; if repair is impossible the item is replaced. Repairs under warranty take up to 21 days. Electronics such as heated insoles have a 1-year warranty on batteries. Claims are submitted through the Warranty form in the customer account. Waterproof jackets are guaranteed waterproof for 3 years. Shipping costs for warranty claims are covered by Acme. Items bought second-hand from other customers are not covered. The warranty is valid in all countries we ship to.
