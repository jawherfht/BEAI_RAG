# Acme Stores – Frequently Asked Questions

## Stores and opening hours

Our stores are open Monday to Saturday from 10 am to 8 pm. Flagship stores in Berlin and Paris are also open on Sundays from 12 to 6 pm. All stores are closed on 25 December and 1 January.

## Payment

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay and Klarna. Klarna lets you pay in 3 interest-free instalments for orders above 100 EUR. Cash is accepted in stores but not for online orders.

## Gift cards

Gift cards are available from 10 to 500 EUR and are valid for 3 years. Gift card balances can be checked on the website with the card number and PIN. Lost gift cards can only be blocked if you have the purchase receipt.

## Acme Plus loyalty programme

Acme Plus is free to join and earns 1 point per euro spent. 500 points can be redeemed for a 10 EUR voucher. Members receive a 15 percent birthday discount valid for one month. Points expire 24 months after they were earned.

## Price match

If you find the same item cheaper at an authorised retailer within 14 days, we refund the difference. Price matching does not apply to marketplace sellers or flash sales.

## Students and staff

Students get 10 percent off with a valid student card in store.

## Customer service

Customer service can be reached at +49 30 1234 5678 or help@acme-retail.example. Emails are answered within 24 hours on business days.
